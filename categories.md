# Budget Categories

## Saving: 10%-15%

- Emergency fund
- Retirement investing
- Down payment for a house
- New car
- Other medium- and long-term goals

## Housing: 25%-35%

- Rent
- Mortgage payment
- Security deposit or initial deposit
- Home repairs and maintenance
- Property taxes
- Homeowners association fees
- Furniture
- Household supplies

## Utilities: 5%-10%

- Electricity
- Water and sewer
- Natural gas, heating oil, or propane
- Internet
- Cell phone
- Trash and recycling

## Food: 10%-15%

- Groceries
- Restaurants
- Takeout and delivery
- School or work lunches
- Coffee and snacks
- Food for guests

## Transportation: 10%-15%

- Car payment
- Gas
- Public transit
- Tolls
- Parking
- Rideshare or taxi
- Routine maintenance
- Unexpected repairs
- Tires
- Oil changes
- Registration, inspection, and license fees
- Vehicle taxes
- Bicycle maintenance

## Health / Medical: 5%-10%

- Doctor visits and copays
- Prescriptions
- Dental visits
- Vision care and glasses
- Therapy or counseling
- Medical devices and supplies
- Over-the-counter medicine
- First-aid supplies
- Gym memberships
- Wellness services

## Insurance: 10%-25%

- Auto insurance
- Homeowners or renters insurance
- Health insurance
- Dental insurance
- Vision insurance
- Term life insurance
- Disability insurance
- Long-term care insurance
- Umbrella insurance
- Insurance deductibles

## Personal / Clothing: 5%-10%

- Clothes
- Shoes
- Haircuts
- Toiletries
- Personal care products
- Laundry and dry cleaning
- Personal spending money

## Recreation / Entertainment: 5%-10%

- Subscriptions
- Cable or streaming services
- Fun money
- Date nights
- Trips and camping
- Fishing
- Movies, concerts, and events
- Books and magazines
- Video games
- Hobbies and crafts
- Sports and recreation fees
- Car upgrades
- Gifts, birthdays, and special occasions

## Other Considerations

- Debt payments
- Student loans
- Credit cards
- Medical debt
- Childcare and child support
- School expenses and extracurricular activities
- Pet food, medication, and veterinary care
- Income taxes not withheld from pay
- Work clothing, supplies, tools, and equipment
- Professional dues and career development
- Charitable, community, or religious giving
- Legal obligations
- Family support
- Bank fees
- Replacement of lost or broken items
- Unexpected fees
- Cash buffer
