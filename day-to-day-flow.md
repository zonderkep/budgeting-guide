# Day-to-Day Budget Flow

Use this process to maintain the budget in Actual Budget.

## Process Transactions

1. Import or sync transactions from the bank each day when possible.
2. Review every imported transaction.
3. Assign the correct category to each transaction.
4. Split a transaction when it includes purchases from more than one category.
5. Check a merchant record, such as an Amazon order, when the transaction is not clear.
6. Assign a cash withdrawal to the category for the intended use of the cash. Do not leave the withdrawal in a general or incorrect category.

Categorize a transaction when you import it, even if it has not cleared. Check uncleared transactions again before you close the month.

## Fund Categories

Review the category list in priority order. Assign available money to the highest-priority category first. Fund each category as far as the available money allows. Then continue to the next category.

Use these rules for common category types:

- **Monthly recurring spending:** Review spending from the previous one or two months. Use this information to set the amount for the current month.
- **Annual recurring spending:** Estimate the next annual cost. A simple estimate is last year's cost plus 10 percent. Divide this amount by 12 and save this amount each month. For example: `(last year's cost + 10%) / 12`.
- **Sinking fund:** Add the planned fixed amount every month. Do this even when the category has no spending in the current month.
- **Capped buffer:** Add money until the category reaches its cap. Put the cap in the category name. For example: `Car repairs - cap $1,000`.

When a capped buffer reaches its cap, stop adding money to it. Assign the available money to the next priority. If spending reduces the buffer below its cap, fund it again when it reaches its place in the priority order.

Over time, several capped buffers may reach their caps. This releases money for other priorities and allows you to make faster progress on them.

## Close the Month

Close the previous month after the new month starts. Do not close the month too early. Some transactions may not have cleared yet.

Close the month when:

- The transactions for the previous month are imported.
- The transactions are categorized.
- Expected transactions have cleared, or you have confirmed that no missing transaction will change the budget.

This often occurs between the second and fifth day of the new month.

Then complete these steps:

1. Review the previous month's transactions one more time.
2. Check for uncleared, missing, or incorrectly categorized transactions.
3. Adjust category priorities when the current situation requires a change.
4. Reconcile the budget. Resolve overspending and unexplained money.
5. Move or assign remaining money according to the next month's priorities.
6. Start the budget process for the new month.

Consistent transaction review and category funding can allow you to budget further ahead over time.
